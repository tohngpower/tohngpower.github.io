Privacy Policy

Wandee AI (Android)
Effective Date: November 5, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how TohngPower ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Wandee AI ("the App").

Wandee AI is an Android application available to users worldwide. By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

Wandee AI does not require you to create an account, register, or provide your name, email address, or other personal information to use its general features.

We do not operate our own account system or collect personal information directly for user registration.

However, third-party services integrated into the App, such as Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Google Play, and AI providers, may collect or process information as described in this policy and their own privacy policies.

1.2 Usage Data and Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with Wandee AI and improve the App.

Firebase Analytics may collect information such as:

  • App usage events and interactions.
  • Features and screens used within the App.
  • App-related technical and diagnostic information.
  • Device and application information used for analytics.
  • App interaction patterns and usage statistics.

The information collected depends on the Firebase Analytics configuration and applicable platform settings.

We use this information to understand feature usage, improve the user experience, identify potential issues, and maintain the functionality of the App.

1.3 Advertising Information

Wandee AI displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process information for advertising purposes, which may include:

  • Device information and advertising identifiers.
  • IP address and general location information derived from it.
  • Advertising interactions and impressions.
  • App and device information relevant to advertising.

The information collected and how it is used may depend on your device settings, applicable consent requirements, and Google's advertising configuration.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through integrated services may be used for the following purposes:

  • Understanding how users interact with Wandee AI.
  • Improving application features, functionality, and user experience.
  • Identifying technical issues and improving application performance.
  • Displaying advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Supporting the operation of third-party AI and other integrated services.

We do not sell users' personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party AI Services

Wandee AI integrates with various third-party AI services and providers to offer features such as AI conversations, voice processing, translation, image generation, image editing, and content understanding.

When you use these features, information you provide may be transmitted to the relevant AI provider for processing. Depending on the feature, this information may include:

  • Text prompts and conversation messages.
  • Images and image-editing instructions.
  • Audio recordings or speech input.
  • Videos and documents submitted for processing.
  • Other information necessary to provide the requested AI service.

AI image generation and image editing may require you to provide your own API key from a supported provider. These features may involve additional charges determined by the provider.

Third-party AI providers may process, retain, or use submitted information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Their data handling practices are outside our control.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the relevant AI providers before using their services, especially when submitting sensitive or confidential information.

4. User-Provided API Keys

Some features allow you to provide your own API keys to access third-party AI services.

API keys provided through the App are stored locally on your Android device. We do not operate our own server to collect or centrally store these API keys.

When you use a feature that requires an API key, the key may be transmitted to the relevant third-party provider to authenticate and process your request.

You are responsible for keeping your API keys secure and managing their usage and associated costs.

5. Local Storage and Offline Data

Wandee AI may store certain information locally on your Android device to provide application functionality and offline access.

This may include:

  • Conversation history and AI-generated content.
  • Notes and reminders.
  • Saved text, images, and audio.
  • User-provided API keys and application settings.
  • Other content created or saved within the App.

Locally stored information is generally managed on your device rather than maintained in a personal cloud account operated by TohngPower.

If you uninstall the App, clear its application data, or reset your device, locally stored information may be permanently deleted.

We do not guarantee that locally stored information can be recovered.

Information you choose to submit to third-party AI services may leave your device and be processed according to those providers' policies.

6. Google Play Purchases

Wandee AI offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to permanently remove advertisements.

Purchases are processed through Google Play. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details.

Google Play may process purchase-related information, such as transaction details and purchase status, in accordance with its own privacy policy.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

Wandee AI uses or integrates with third-party services to provide its functionality.

These may include:

  • Firebase Analytics: For application usage analytics.

These services are operated by independent providers and may collect or process information under their own privacy policies and terms.
